# Facebook Launch Kit — Tools Stars

A complete, copy-paste social promotion kit. Everything here is **external to the
website** — none of it changes toolsstars.com, so it is 100% safe during the
AdSense review window. All post copy is in English (wider reach + matches site
language).

> **Golden rule while AdSense review is pending:** promote freely on Facebook,
> but do NOT publish new articles/pages on the site, and do NOT click any ads
> that appear on the site once ads go live (that is the one true ban risk).

---

## 1. How Facebook actually works (honest version)

- **Page organic reach is small.** A new page's posts are seen by very few
  people. The three levers that actually work: **Groups**, **Reels**, and
  **image posts with real before/after proof**.
- **Facebook suppresses posts with external links in the body.** Put the link
  in the **first comment** and say "link in comments" in the post.
- **Real screenshots beat stock graphics.** Use actual before/after screenshots
  taken from your own phone/browser while using the tools. Never present
  fake/AI-generated images as tool results.
- **Spam filters are real.** Do NOT paste identical text into 20 groups in one
  day — Facebook will restrict the account. Vary the wording, max 2–3 group
  shares per day.
- **Never claim** "world's best", "#1", "100% accurate". Keep claims modest and
  true — this also keeps the site's reputation clean for AdSense.

## 2. Setup (one time, ~15 minutes)

1. Create a Facebook **Page** named **Tools Stars** (not just your personal
   profile). Category: Website / Software.
2. Profile picture: the site logo/favicon. Cover: a simple banner —
   "50+ free file tools. Nothing is ever uploaded."
3. About section: short description + website `https://toolsstars.com`.
4. Every link you share should carry a UTM tag so GA4 shows Facebook traffic:

   ```
   https://toolsstars.com/tools/<slug>?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<slug>
   https://toolsstars.com/blog/<slug>?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=blog-<slug>
   ```

   Check results in GA4 → Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition →
   session source = facebook.

## 4. Group strategy (the real traffic source)

Join 10–15 groups in these niches (search on Facebook):

- Photo editing / Photoshop alternatives / Canva users
- Freelancers (designers, virtual assistants)
- Small business / online sellers (product photos!)
- Students & job seekers (CVs, application forms, PDF conversions)
- E-commerce sellers (Amazon/Daraz/Shopify-style groups — product shots)

**How to post in groups without getting banned:**

1. Read each group's rules first. Some allow promotion only on certain days or
   in pinned "self-promo" threads — follow that.
2. The highest-converting move is not posting ads; it's **answering questions**.
   When someone asks "how do I remove a background for free?", reply helpfully
   and mention the tool. Moderators allow this; cold link-drops get removed.
3. Max 2–3 group shares per day, each with slightly different wording.
4. Track which groups send clicks (GA4) and double down on those.

## 7. Honest expectations

- First days/weeks: small numbers (tens of clicks per good post). Reach
  compounds as the page ages and groups recognize you.
- Facebook traffic does not pay directly until AdSense is approved (no ads are
  being served yet). What it DOES do now: proves the site is active, seeds
  repeat visitors, and tells us which tools resonate — which shapes the
  article plan after approval.
- One good group post or reel that hits the right niche (e.g. sellers needing
  product photos) can outperform everything else combined. Watch GA4 and
  double down on what works.
- Do not buy likes/followers — fake engagement destroys reach and looks
  scammy to both Facebook and Google.
